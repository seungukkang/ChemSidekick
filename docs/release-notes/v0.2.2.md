# v0.2.2 · 2026-11-27

A new category, Biochemistry, with its first tool: an amino-acid reference.

## Added

### `aa` (BIO-01)

- The 20 standard amino acids plus selenocysteine (Sec, U) and pyrrolysine (Pyl, O), grouped by side-chain class: nonpolar aliphatic, aromatic, polar uncharged, positively charged, negatively charged.
- Look one up by one-letter code, three-letter code, or name (`csk aa W`, `csk aa Trp`, `csk aa tryptophan`), or list a class (`csk aa aromatic`, `csk aa basic`). `csk aa` lists all of them.
- The ambiguity codes B (Asx), Z (Glx), J (Xle) and X list the amino acids they stand for.
- Each card shows:
  - codes, name, IUPAC name, class, and whether it is essential in humans
  - formula, SMILES (neutral and the pH 7 form), InChIKey, and CIP labels (L · (2S); Cys and Sec are (2R))
  - monoisotopic and average masses, of the free amino acid and of the residue in a peptide
  - α-carboxyl, α-amino and side-chain pKa, and the pI
  - Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy
  - codons in the standard genetic code
- **Drawing:** the pH 7 form with the backbone in the same place and at the same scale for every amino acid, so only the side chain changes. The side chain is highlighted in its class color, and the card border uses that color too.
- **3D:** ideal coordinates with hydrogens from the wwPDB Chemical Component Dictionary (CCD), in the interactive viewer.
- **Data:** structures, formulas and masses are checked against the CCD, PubChem and ChEBI. Codons are from NCBI translation table 1. Hydropathy values come from Kyte and Doolittle (1982) and match the ExPASy ProtScale copy. The report is in [`data/verification/amino_acids.md`](../../data/verification/amino_acids.md).

### Raycast extension

- **Amino Acids:** names and codes grouped by class on the left, the drawing and key values on the right. Choose the order beside the search bar: by class, alphabetical, by residue mass, or by hydropathy.
  - ⌘I shows the full card.
  - ⌘O opens the 3D structure in the browser.
  - ⌘G shows every drawing as a grid.
  - ⌘C copies the monoisotopic residue mass. The codes, the average mass and the SMILES can be copied from the action panel.
- The ChemSidekick command opens `aa` in the same view.

## Known issues

- pKa values come from one textbook table (Lehninger, 7th ed.) and are marked provisional. They are not checked against other sources yet, and published values differ by 0.1–0.5 units. The pI is computed from them.
- Hydropathy values have two references (the original paper and ExPASy), not three, so they are shown as ≈.
- The CCD stores Lys, His and Arg with a protonated side chain, so their 3D structures carry that extra hydrogen. The card notes this.
- `csk ui` shows `aa` on the standard result page. A page made for it will come later.
- Peptide sequences (`ACDE`) are not supported yet.
- v0.2.1's known issues still apply.
- Tested on macOS only.
