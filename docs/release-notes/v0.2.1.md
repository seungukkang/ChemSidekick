# v0.2.1 · 2026-11-14

Two new tools: chemistry unit conversions and energy profiles (rates, ratios, populations).

## Added

### `units` (REF-02)

- Converts the chemistry units a calculator doesn't: kcal/mol ↔ kJ/mol ↔ eV ↔ hartree, cm⁻¹ ↔ nm ↔ K, molar entropy, bohr ↔ Å, debye, polarizability, M⁻¹ s⁻¹ ↔ cm³ molecule⁻¹ s⁻¹, pressure, and charge.
- `csk units 1 eV to kcal/mol` gives one conversion. `csk units 300 nm` lists every related unit.
- Shows RT and kT at the temperature you set (`-T 25 C`).
- Each conversion shows its definition, the constants it uses, and their sources. Results are given to 6 significant figures.
- Conversions that use only defined constants are marked exact. The measured constants (hartree, Bohr radius, ε₀; CODATA 2022) are each checked against three references.

### `profile` (ENE-01)

- **Reaction:** an energy profile A → TS → products with one or more competing paths. Enter the barrier ΔG‡, a rate constant k, or a half-life for each TS, and ΔG or K for each product.
- It gives:
  - rate constants, half-lives, and the time to 90 % and 99 % conversion
  - kinetic and equilibrium product ratios
  - reverse barriers and the equilibrium share of each product
- **Options:**
  - Unimolecular or bimolecular; activation- or diffusion-controlled, with a solvent viscosity.
  - Energies from a gas-phase calculation (1 atm) are converted to 1 M.
  - Wigner tunneling correction from an imaginary frequency.
- **Targets:** the temperature for a given half-life, and the ΔΔG‡ needed for a given selectivity (95:5, 90 % ee, 10:1 dr).
- For each path, the temperatures at which the half-life is 1 min, 1 h, and 1 day.
- **Populations:** Boltzmann populations of states (conformers), with degeneracies and absolute energies in hartree. Shows which states make up 95 % and averages any properties you add.
- **Drawing:** the energy profile or population ladder, with the formulas used typeset below it.
- **Warnings:** a barrier below 5RT, where the Eyring rate is only qualitative; a negative barrier; a TS below its product; a rate near the diffusion limit.
- Computed values are shown to 3 significant figures. Calculations use the values as entered.
- Solvent viscosities are listed only for water, methanol, toluene, and benzene, where three references agree. For other solvents, type the viscosity.

### Raycast extension

- **Convert Units:** pick a unit group, type a value, and pick its unit. Every unit in the group is listed with its definition and sources. Enter copies a value; ⌘⇧C copies it at full precision.
- **Energy Profile:** paths on the left; the drawing and answer on the right, redrawn as you type.
  - Type `D 21 -4` (name, TS, product) to add a path.
  - Pick reaction or populations beside the search bar.
  - ⌘↵ shows the full result.

### Web interface

- `csk ui` has a live Energy Profile page: input on the left (paste paths from a spreadsheet), drawing and results on the right.

## Changed

- In `--json` output, each formula has an `in_drawing` field: `true` when the formula is already typeset in the drawing.
- The Raycast extension needs `csk` reinstalled after this update: `uv tool install --editable . --reinstall`.

## Known issues

- Diffusion-controlled rates use 25 °C solvent viscosities, so they are approximate at other temperatures (a warning says so).
- Paths are A → B only; multi-step mechanisms are not supported yet.
- Raycast's populations view shows names, energies and degeneracies only. Use `csk` or `csk ui` for property averages.
- v0.2.0's known issues still apply.
- Tested on macOS only.
